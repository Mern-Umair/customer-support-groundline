# LinkedIn targeting plan

Goal: be seen by the people who hire remote engineers for AI-support products and by the clients who post this work on Upwork. Audience is abroad (US, UK, EU, UAE), not Pakistan.

## 1. Who to target (five rings)

### Ring A — founders of the products Groundline is a small version of (verified 8 Oct 2026)
| Company | Person | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chatbase | Yasser Elsaid | Founder & CEO | Bootstrapped the exact product; posts about building it |
| Botpress | Sylvain Perron | CEO | Agent platform; developer-focused |
| Voiceflow | Braden Ream | Co-founder & CEO | Conversational AI; active poster |
| Crisp | Baptiste Jamin, Valerian Saliou | Co-founders | Bootstrapped messaging + AI, European |
| Tidio | Tytus Gołas | Co-founder & CEO | Lyro AI, SMB support, Poland/EU |
| Intercom (Fin) | Des Traynor (co-founder), Fergal Reid (AI lead) | Confirm current titles on LinkedIn | Fin is the benchmark; both write about AI support |

Also: founders of smaller AI-support startups found via "Ring E" searches.

### Ring B — engineers and engineering managers at those companies
Search: company page → People → filter title. Target: senior/staff engineers, engineering managers, "AI engineer", "ML engineer", "Applied AI". These people comment on each other's posts and are the ones who refer candidates.

### Ring C — hiring side
Recruiters and talent partners at the same companies, plus remote-first AI startups. Titles: "Technical Recruiter", "Talent Partner", "Head of Talent", "Engineering Recruiter".

### Ring D — buyers (Upwork clients and agency owners)
Agency owners and ops leads at small e-commerce / SaaS companies in UAE, UK, NL, DE who post about "AI support", "chatbot for our website", "automate customer support". They hire freelancers.

### Ring E — the AI-engineering conversation
People who post weekly about RAG, evals, LLM apps, with 5k–100k followers. Commenting here early gives reach. Find via hashtag/keyword search on posts (see §2).

Target size: 150–200 follows in the first two weeks, then 10 new per week.

## 2. How to find them (exact searches)

LinkedIn search bar → type the query → pick the **People** or **Posts** tab.

People searches (use the filters: Locations = United States, United Kingdom, Germany, Netherlands, United Arab Emirates; Current company when relevant):
- `"AI engineer" customer support`
- `"applied AI" chatbot`
- `RAG engineer`
- `"customer support" AI founder`
- `"head of support" AI`
- `"Intercom" engineer` (then filter Current company = Intercom; repeat for Chatbase, Tidio, Botpress, Crisp, Voiceflow, Ada, Forethought, Zendesk, Freshworks, Kustomer, Gorgias, HubSpot)
- `technical recruiter AI startup remote`

Post searches (Posts tab, sort by **Latest**, read daily):
- `#buildinpublic RAG`
- `"AI support" launch`
- `"customer support" "AI agent" we built`
- `evals LLM production`
- `"hands off to a human"` / `"human handoff"`
- `Upwork "AI chatbot" documents` (clients sometimes post the same brief on LinkedIn)

Company pages → **People** tab → filter by keyword "engineer" or "recruiter". This is the fastest way to fill Ring B and C.

Other sources:
- Comment sections of Ring A posts: everyone who comments thoughtfully there is a Ring B/E candidate.
- Y Combinator "Work at a Startup" (workatastartup.com): filter Remote + AI; follow the founders of companies posting support/agent roles.
- Upwork postings you apply to: look up the client's company on LinkedIn, follow the poster.
- Podcasts/talks by Ring A people: speakers and hosts at the same events are Ring E.

## 3. What to do with them (daily, 20 minutes)

1. Follow, do not connect-request cold. Follow 10–15 people a day.
2. Comment on 3–5 posts a day from Rings A, B, E. A real comment: one observation from your own build ("we hit the same thing: Readability dropped short FAQ pages, so we fall back to body text") or a specific question. Never "Great post!".
3. After 2–3 comments the person has seen, send a connection request with one line: "Building a small open-source version of what you ship, posting weekly, would value your eye on it." No ask for a job.
4. Reply to every comment on your own posts within the first hour; it drives reach.
5. Weekly post goes out Tuesday or Wednesday, 9–11 am UK time (Europe awake, US east coast starting).

## 4. What to send when it is time to ask

Only after a live demo exists (week 4+). Message, not post:
"Hi X, I built Groundline, a small open-source take on [their product]: RAG with citations, live human handoff, and a public evals page. Live: <url>, code: <repo>. If your team ever needs a remote engineer who has shipped this end to end, I'd love a conversation." Attach nothing; the link is the attachment.

## 5. Tracking

Keep a simple sheet (Google Sheets is fine): name · company · ring · date followed · comments made · connected? · replied? Review every Sunday. The number that matters is replies from Rings A–C, not follower count.

## 6. Do not

- Tag people in posts you have not interacted with before.
- Message recruiters before the demo is live.
- Post in Urdu or about Pakistan-specific context on this account.
- Invent numbers. Every claim in a post must be reproducible from the repo.
