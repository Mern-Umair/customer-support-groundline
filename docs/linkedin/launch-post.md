I built an AI customer-support platform from scratch. It's live today.

Groundline answers your customers' questions from your own website and documents, with a source citation on every answer. When it can't answer, it says so and hands the chat to a real person in real time. No guessing.

For a business, it's three steps:
1. Add your website URL or PDFs.
2. Paste one script tag on your site.
3. Visitors get cited answers. Your team gets a live inbox and a list of every question the docs couldn't answer.

Measured, not promised (public evals, 40-question test set):
• Answer accuracy: 87%
• Cited the right document: 100%
• Correct refusals on unanswerable questions: 100%
• Cost per answer: under $0.001

Built with Next.js 16, TypeScript, MongoDB Atlas Vector Search, Gemini, Socket.io, Stripe. 150+ automated tests, CI on every push, running on free tiers.
